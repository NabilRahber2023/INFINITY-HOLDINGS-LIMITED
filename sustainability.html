(Sustainability)

Growth the planet can keep.

(Our Commitment)

We reject the idea that growth and responsibility are in tension. A company built to last a century has no choice but to protect the world it will operate in — so sustainability is not a department at Infinity. It is the operating model.

Net-zero across all operations by 2040 — and a portfolio that gives more than it takes long before then.

(The Roadmap)

Measurable
milestones.

No vague pledges. A dated path, reported publicly, and tied to how our leaders are rewarded.

2025

50% renewable

Half of the group's total energy demand met by our own solar, wind and storage assets — a target we are on track to exceed.

2030

Every new building net-zero

All new developments designed to operate at net-zero carbon, with embodied-carbon budgets applied from the first sketch.

2035

Circular by default

Manufacturing and construction shift to reused and low-carbon materials, with waste-to-landfill approaching zero.

2040

Net-zero group-wide

Net-zero across all scopes, independently verified — the destination the entire roadmap has been built to reach.

(Clean Power)

We power
ourselves first.

Infinity Energy exists so the rest of the group can decarbonise without waiting for the wider grid. Every solar field and wind farm we build serves our own portfolio before it serves the market — proof that the transition is not only necessary, but profitable.
