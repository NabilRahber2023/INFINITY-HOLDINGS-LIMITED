(Who We Are)

Infinity Holdings Limited is a privately held, globally diversified holding company. For more than a quarter of a century we have built, acquired and stewarded businesses across seven industries — always with the same intent: to create value that endures for generations.

(Our Story)

From a single
idea to seven
industries.

Growth, for us, has never meant sprawl. Each chapter deepened a conviction that great companies are built patiently, and held with care.

1998

The founding

Infinity is established as a real-estate developer with a single conviction — that the built environment deserves to be crafted with intention rather than merely constructed.

2006

Into infrastructure & hospitality

The group expands beyond property into the systems that support cities and the places that welcome the world — laying the foundations of a diversified holding company.

2014

Capital & technology

Infinity Investments and a dedicated technology arm are formed, turning the group's balance sheet and data into engines of compounding advantage.

2019

The energy transition

Infinity Energy launches with a mandate to power the portfolio — and the wider grid — from renewable sources, setting a course toward net-zero.

Today

Seven industries, one vision

Forty operating companies across four continents, held under a single roof, united by the belief that everything we build should be designed for life.

We do not chase the quarter. We build for the century.
— The Infinity Charter
